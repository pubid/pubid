[[urn-gost]]
= GOST URN schemes

This file is the single home of the GOST (`gost`) flavor's URN
documentation — the per-flavor split of the former `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`
(owner ruling 2026-11-25: "for URN schemes how can we mix up different
flavors in 1 file? SPLIT THE FILE!"). The index that links every flavor's
file is `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`.

Namespace: `urn:pubid:gost:`. Schema: `lib/pubid/gost/pubid_urn_schema.rb`.

Official URN: none. GOST has no namespace in the IANA registry, and the
current `urn:gost:…` form of `to_urn` is pubid-invented (legacy, no
round-trip promise). Current `to_urn`: 200 ids, 77 round-trip, 123 reshape,
9 colliding URNs.

pubid scheme: all 200 pass ids emit, round-trip by `to_hash`, are path-safe,
and give no collision. They give 199 URNs, because `ГОСТ Р 71039—2023` and
`ГОСТ Р 71039— 2023` are one document with one `to_hash`. The longest URN is
181 characters (a harmonized standard that nests a consolidated EN).

[cols="3,1",options="header"]
|===
| `_type` | token
| `pubid:gost:interstate-standard` | `is`
| `pubid:gost:national-standard` | `ns`
| `pubid:gost:harmonized` | `h`
| `pubid:gost:identical-adoption` | `ia`
| `pubid:gost:foreign-reference` | `fr`
| `pubid:gost:identifier` | `id`
|===

Core: `number`. Atoms, in the schema order: `pub-`, `co-` (`copublisher`),
`year-`, `subtype-`, `raw-`, `adoptedidentifiers-` (`adopted_identifiers`,
ids), `adopted-` (id). No facets.

A harmonized or identical adoption holds the GOST standard in `base`, and its
`to_hash` repeats that standard's number and year, so the URN writes them
twice: once in the base node, once in the adoption node. The adopted
documents nest with their own flavor's schema: `astm`, `cencenelec`, `iec`,
`ieee`, `iso`. `cencenelec` has a *partial* schema for this wave (see
<<../cen_cenelec/urn.adoc#urn-cen_cenelec,CEN/CENELEC>>).

----
ГОСТ ISO 6848                          urn:pubid:gost:is:6848:co-ISO
ГОСТ Р 58904-2020/ISO/TR 25901-1:2016  urn:pubid:gost:ns:58904:year-2020:ia:58904:year-2020:adopted-(iso:tr:25901:part-1:year-2016)
ГОСТ 1437-2024 (ASTM D129-18)          urn:pubid:gost:is:1437:year-2024:h:1437:year-2024:adoptedidentifiers-(astm:std:129:letter-D:year-2018)
----

Known gap, pinned by `spec/pubid/gost/pubid_urn_spec.rb`: a
`ForeignReference` (`(OECD 460:2017)`, the form for a document of a flavor
pubid does not know) keeps its text in `raw`, which `to_hash` does not write.
Two adoptions of different unknown documents therefore have one hash and one
URN, and `from_hash` renders `GOST 34853-2022 (OECD 460:2017)` as
`GOST 34853-2022`. The fallback is wider than unknown flavors: a known
document that the adopted flavor cannot parse also becomes a
`ForeignReference` (`(EN 1234:2010-05)`, `(CEN/CENELEC EN 1234:2010)`,
`(EN 1234:2010/prA1)`). The fix (map `raw` in `key_value`) changes `to_hash`, so it
needs a `pubid-testsuite` update and ships separately; the `raw-` atom is
already declared for it. `relaton-data-gost` holds no foreign-reference row.

Found in passing, not fixed: `ГОСТ 35294.2-2025 (ISO 8124-2: 2023)` (a space
after the colon) routes its adopted document to IEEE and drops the year:
`to_s` is `GOST 35294.2-2025 (ISO 8124-2)`.

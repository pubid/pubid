= CEN PFS URN schemes

This file is the single home of the CEN PFS (`cen_pfs`) flavor's URN
documentation — the per-flavor split of the former `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`
(owner ruling 2026-11-25: "for URN schemes how can we mix up different
flavors in 1 file? SPLIT THE FILE!"). The index that links every flavor's
file is `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`.

The PFS-compiled module-composition surface of cen_cenelec
(pubid/pubid#706 §4 — the NSB port). The composed definition models the
published EN face and declares the scheme-canonical tag
(`pubid:cencenelec:european-norm`) with the atoms of the composed model
(`part-`, `year-`); its URN parity with the coded flavor on to_urn /
to_mr_string is the measured boundary (the typed-stage machinery is
blocker 2 of `definitions/cen.pfs`), while to_pubid_urn emits and
round-trips over the absorbed rows. Its literal corpus is the
measurement harness's absorbed faces (`spec/pubid/cen_pfs/`).

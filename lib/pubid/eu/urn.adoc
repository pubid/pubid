[[urn-eu]]
= EU URN schemes

This file is the single home of the EU (`eu`) flavor's URN
documentation — the per-flavor split of the former `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`
(owner ruling 2026-11-25: "for URN schemes how can we mix up different
flavors in 1 file? SPLIT THE FILE!"). The index that links every flavor's
file is `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`.

Namespace: `urn:pubid:eu:` (the `_type` flavor segment).

Official URN: none. The EU has no namespace in the IANA registry; its official identifier is the ELI (`http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1227/oj`, `#to_eli`). The `urn:eu:<eli type>:<year>:<number>` form of `to_urn` is pubid-invented.

Corpus: 28 of 35 full ids parse (the 7 fail rows are a bare number, a pair with no year, two two-digit-year acts, two conflicting families, a year 0000 CELEX and an unknown ELI subtype).

[cols="3,1,1,1,4",options="header"]
|===
| `_type` | token | core | ids | example
| `pubid:eu:regulation` | `reg` | year, number | 14 | `EU Regulation 1227/2011`
| `pubid:eu:directive` | `dir` | year, number | 6 | `EU Directive 2016/680`
| `pubid:eu:decision` | `dec` | year, number | 4 | `EU Decision 2010/87`
|===

The core is year first, the CELEX and ELI order, whatever order the act prints in.

Atoms, canonical order: `family-` (`family`, the bracketed legal family `EU`/`EC`/`EEC`/`Euratom`/`CFSP`).

Facets: `numberfirst-` (`number_first`, bool) — the print order when it differs from the default rule; spelling only, so it is cut from identity (`urn:pubid:eu:dec:2013:1082:f:0.numberfirst-true`); `subtype-` (`subtype`, string) — the ELI type subtype `impl`/`del`/`financ` of a `reg_impl`-style ELI; it names no other act, so it is cut from identity (`urn:pubid:eu:reg:2016:1356:f:0.subtype-impl`).

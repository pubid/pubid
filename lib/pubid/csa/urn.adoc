[[urn-csa]]
= CSA URN schemes

This file is the single home of the CSA (`csa`) flavor's URN
documentation — the per-flavor split of the former `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`
(owner ruling 2026-11-25: "for URN schemes how can we mix up different
flavors in 1 file? SPLIT THE FILE!"). The index that links every flavor's
file is `docs/URN-SCHEMES.adoc`.

Namespace: `urn:pubid:csa:` (the `_type` flavor segment).

Official URN: none. The organization has no namespace in the IANA registry, and the current `urn:csa:…` form of `to_urn` is pubid-invented (legacy, no round-trip promise).

Current `to_urn` (≤400 sampled pass ids): 400 ids, 9 round-trip, 77 reshape, 22 do not re-parse, 292 raise, 0 empty, 0 colliding URNs.

Corpus: 829 of 829 pass ids parse.

Atoms, canonical order: `series-` (`series`, bool), `year-` (`year`), `reaff-` (`reaffirmation`), `cecpart-` (`cec_part`), `codeonly-` (`code_only`, bool), `materialsafterkeyword-` (`materials_after_keyword`, bool), `nonumber-` (`no_number`), `packagekeyword-` (`package_keyword`), `packagematerials-` (`package_materials`).

Nested identifiers: `bundledwith-(…)` (`bundled_with`, ids), `ids-(…)` (`identifiers`, ids).

Facet candidates (spelling-only): `original_reaffirmation_4digit`, `year_format`, `has_publisher`, `publisher_prefix`, `year_prefix`, `original_year_4digit`, `separator`, `series_prefix`.

`french` was a facet when the schema landed and is an atom now (`french-`,
bool, after `bundledwith-`): `CSA B149.1:F20` is the French edition, another
publication than `CSA B149.1:20`, so identity compares it (issue #656). The
`F` it is printed with stays the `year_prefix` facet.

Nested identifiers of other flavors (written with their own schema, qualified token `<flavor>.<token>`): `iec`, `iso`.

*Landed (issue #670) as drafted*, with `original_year_4digit` the bool the
corpus serializes, and the wrapper precedents: a combined identifier's
members are `ids-`, a bundled one's followers `bundledwith-`, and an
adoption's document goes along `base`, keeping its own flavor's schema
(`CSA ISO/IEC 8824-1:22` is
`urn:pubid:csa:iso.is:8824:co-IEC:part-1:year-2022:ca2`). All 829 corpus
rows emit.
